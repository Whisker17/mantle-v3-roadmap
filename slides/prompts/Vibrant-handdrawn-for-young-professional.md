Based on selected material, generates slides. Ask user how many pages they want then proceed. 1. Visual Philosophy & Mood

The core philosophy is "De-institutionalization." It deliberately shuns the sterile perfection of smooth vector lines and generic stock imagery in favor of an honest, warm, and tactile aesthetic reminiscent of a startup’s culture or an independent indie magazine (Zine). It strikes a sophisticated balance: injecting humanistic care into professional productivity.

Keywords: Humanistic, Approachable, Artistic, Energetic, Imperfectly Authentic.

2. Color System & Materiality

The deck utilizes a high-contrast tri-color system.

Base: Off-white / Ivory — Softer than pure white, providing a paper-like texture.

Primary: Muted Yellow — Large blocks of color symbolize sunlight, energy, and focus.

Accent: Vibrant Pink — Used for granular details (lightbulbs, arrows, checkmarks) to guide the visual center of gravity.

Outline: Charcoal Black — A "soft" black with the grainy texture of a hand-drawn brush.

Materiality: Mimics the effect of marker pens on paper, featuring slight color "bleeding" and intentional white gaps to create "breathing room."

3. Layout Logic & Grid

Asymmetric Dynamic Balance.

Split Structure: Typically features a bold text headline on one side balanced by a large-scale character illustration on the other.

Negative Space: Significant use of white space; it doesn't feel compelled to fill every corner, ensuring the core message pops.

Alignment: Primarily left-aligned or centered with a loose, airy arrangement that mimics a poster layout rather than a traditional corporate presentation.

4. Typography Personality

Headline: A Display Handwritten Font with serif tendencies. The varying stroke thickness gives it a rustic, woodcut, or stamped feel, adding a sense of raw power to the screen.

Body: A modern, minimalist neutral Sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat or Open Sans). It provides a rational, legible anchor against the more emotional, illustrative background.

5. Graphic Elements & Language

Illustrative Language: Bold Outlines with slightly exaggerated human proportions (larger hands/feet). Facial expressions are hyper-simplified (minimalist line-drawn smiles).

Metaphorical Symbols: Minimalist icons (Lightbulb = Idea, Envelope = Communication, Gear = Mechanism) are brought to life through the Vibrant Pink accent.

Line Quality: Trembling, unclosed lines that convey the visual excitement of "spontaneous creation."

6. Visual Hierarchy & Depth

Flat Design 2.0: Zero shadows and zero gradients. Depth is achieved entirely through the layering of solid color blocks and outlines to separate foreground from background.

Visual Guiding: Uses pink "arrows" or "underlines" as signposts to create a clear "reading trajectory."

Characters as Anchors: Every slide features human action (drinking coffee, high-fiving, discussing), which serves to ground abstract concepts in reality.

7. Scenarios & Industry Alignment

Perfectly suited for AI tech firms, creative studios, mental health platforms, ESG education, or workplace culture transformations. It aligns seamlessly with the "Humanistic Texture" of brand building—positioning a company as empathetic, warm, and decidedly "un-corporate."
